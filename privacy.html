Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Reels Pipeline, a personal automation tool, handles data when publishing content to connected social media accounts.

1. Data Collected

Reels Pipeline processes only the data strictly necessary to generate and publish video content: narration audio, generated video files, captions, hashtags, and OAuth access/refresh tokens issued by connected platforms (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube) for the single account authorizing the connection.

2. Data Use

Collected data is used exclusively to produce and publish video content to the authorized account. No data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than operating this tool.

3. Data Storage

OAuth tokens and related credentials are stored securely within the automation platform (Make) used to run this pipeline, and are not shared with any third party beyond the destination platforms themselves.

4. Third-Party Platforms

When publishing content, this tool interacts with TikTok, Instagram and YouTube through their official APIs. Each platform's own privacy policy governs how they handle the data transmitted to them.

5. Data Retention

Access and refresh tokens are retained only as long as needed to operate the pipeline and can be revoked at any time by the account owner through the respective platform's settings.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the tool's owner directly.
