Reels Pipeline

Automated content publishing pipeline

Reels Pipeline is an internal automation tool that generates short-form educational video content and publishes it to connected social media accounts (including TikTok, Instagram and YouTube) on behalf of its owner, after manual review and approval of each piece of content.

The TikTok integration uses the Content Posting API to publish approved videos directly to the authorized creator's own TikTok account. No content is published without explicit human approval before each upload.
